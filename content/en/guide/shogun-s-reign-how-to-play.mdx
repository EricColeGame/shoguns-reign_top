Set in Edo-period Japan, [Shogun's Reign](https://www.roblox.com/games/106568491289620/Shoguns-Reign) is a Roblox roleplaying and progression experience where you begin with almost nothing and try to climb a social ladder that ends in a single seat: the Shogun. That premise is unusual. Most progression games on the platform reward you with stats and gear, while this one frames advancement as reputation and rank inside a society. According to the official Roblox page, you earn your first Ryō through farming, labour and trades, explore towns, villages, shrines, estates and roads, master a profession, and build wealth and reputation until you can compete for authority. Crucially, the same page does not publish a profession list, rank requirements, pay rates, or a housing system, and it lists no promo codes. This guide sticks to what is documented, flags what is community observation, and explains how to verify the rest yourself in game.

## Starting out: turning labour into Ryō

The official description names farming, labour and trades as the path to your first Ryō, so early play is about labour rather than combat or status. A sensible first session looks like this:

1. Learn the map. Walk the roads between a town and its surrounding farmland or work areas so you stop paying for confusion later.
2. Pick one activity and repeat it. Consistency teaches you the real time-to-payment for each job, which no guide can measure as accurately as you can in your own session.
3. Watch your reputation, not just your purse. Ryō is described as wealth; reputation is described separately, which suggests they are tracked as different things.
4. Talk to other players. In roleplay experiences, other players are frequently the fastest route to understanding unwritten conventions.

If you want a single habit that pays off, keep a short note of what each activity paid and how long it took. That personal log will beat any borrowed estimate.

## The social ladder: servants, warriors, nobles, and one Shogun

The advertised hierarchy runs from servant-level beginnings toward warrior, noble, and feudal lord, with competition for the single position of Shogun at the top. Two implications follow from that wording.

First, highest rank is contested, not earned by everyone. A single Shogun means the ceiling is exclusive, so the realistic long-term goals for most players are the tiers below it.

Second, rank and profession are described as separate ideas. You master a profession, and you climb ranks. Whether a given profession accelerates a given rank is not documented, so treat any claim that "X profession guarantees Y rank" as unverified.

Because requirements are unpublished, verify in game by opening whatever rank, reputation, or profile interface exists, reading the in-game help or tutorial text, and checking the official Roblox page for updates. Since the place page showed a recent update, mechanics may shift between sessions.
