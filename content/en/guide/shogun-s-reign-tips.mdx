## Starting out: earning your first Ryō

The advertised loop is straightforward: you start with almost nothing and earn your first Ryō through farming, labour and trades. In practice, that means the early game is about showing up and doing work rather than chasing rare items.

Practical reasoning for new players:

- Orient before you optimise. Walk towns, villages, shrines, estates and roads so you know where services and gathering spots are. The official page advertises exploration as a feature; use it.
- Treat your first Ryō as a buffer, not a score. Early money is best spent on reducing friction in whatever activity you can repeat reliably.
- Expect the pace to be deliberately slow. Progression games in this genre gate advancement behind time, reputation and roleplay rather than pure grinding.

Because the official page does not publish pay rates, do not trust any number quoted elsewhere. Verify in-game by doing a single task and watching your Ryō change.

## Professions and roleplay: what works in practice

The official description advertises professions you can master, but it does not list them. Community guides and player discussion are the only sources for specifics, and they change over time. Rather than memorise a list, learn how to choose.

Player practice, not documented mechanics:

- Pick a profession that fits the character you are roleplaying, not just the fastest earner. In a persistent-world roleplaying game, consistency makes you recognisable.
- Ask before you act in someone's shop or estate. Uninvited behaviour reads as poor etiquette and can cost you standing with the people who gate opportunities.
- Offer labour before asking for favours. Established players are more likely to mentor newcomers who have already shown they contribute.
- Keep a simple character story — a trade, a home region, a reason for being in town. It makes interactions easier and gives you something to build reputation around.
- Take losses gracefully. If a rank or role goes to someone else, the game continues, and reputation is built over sessions, not moments.

The most accurate way to confirm which professions exist and how they are accessed is to explore in-game and read any in-game menus or notices — not to rely on external lists that may be outdated.
