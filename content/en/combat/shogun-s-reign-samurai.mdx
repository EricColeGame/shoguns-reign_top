In a game with one top slot, raw grinding alone rarely wins — visibility, relationships, and reliability matter enormously. Players who become known as dependable workers, fair traders, or useful allies tend to get pulled into opportunities that pure solo grinding misses.

Practical reasoning follows from that. Build reputation deliberately: finish what you start, honour deals, and avoid burning bridges over small sums of Ryō.
