## The Early Game: Earning Your First Ryō

Because the official description names farming and labour as your entry point, the sensible opening strategy is to treat your first session as a job hunt rather than an adventure. Explore the town or village you spawn near, find where basic work is offered, and repeat it until you have a working balance. Resist the urge to wander toward estates or the road network while broke; the advertised exploration content is more rewarding once you can afford to travel and participate.

A few practical habits help here. Learn the map layout of one settlement before moving to the next. Note which areas are busy with other players, since crowded spaces are usually where roleplay and player-run commerce happen. And keep your expectations loose about income, because the developer has not published pay rates for any activity. If you want a reliable number, time a few work cycles yourself and compare.
