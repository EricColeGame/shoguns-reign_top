## The Loop: Ryō First, Status Later

The core loop the developers describe is refreshingly blunt. You do not pick a starting class or inherit a role. You work. Farming and labour produce your first Ryō, and trades are listed alongside them as a path to income. Only after that does the broader progression layer, professions, reputation and rank, come into play.

This sequencing is the most important thing for a new player to internalise. A player who spends their first hour asking how to become a samurai is wasting time. A player who spends that hour farming has Ryō, and Ryō is the documented prerequisite for everything the page advertises.

## Roleplay Etiquette as Player Practice

Shogun's Reign is a roleplaying game, so how you behave in character affects how far you get socially. The following is player practice, not documented mechanics, so treat it as advice rather than rules.

- Address players in period-appropriate language when you are in character. It signals that you are there to roleplay, not to grind silently.
- Ask before touching another player's property, crops or animals. Owners notice.
- Do not beg for Ryō in spawn areas. It is the fastest way to be ignored by the players who could actually employ you.
- Offer labour before asking for rank. Established players hand out work to people who have already shown up.
- When you are unsure, ask in general chat where the current work is. It changes as the game updates.

None of this guarantees advancement. It simply raises the odds that other players will bring you into their towns, estates and businesses, which is where reputation tends to accumulate fastest.
