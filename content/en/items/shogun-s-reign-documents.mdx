## Roleplay Etiquette Worth Knowing

Roleplay advice below is player practice, not documented mechanics. It reflects how experienced roleplayers generally approach period settings, and it applies to Shogun's Reign as a social environment:

- Stay in character in public spaces like towns, roads, and shrines. Breaking character in a busy area disrupts scenes for everyone.
- Ask before joining an ongoing scene. A short out-of-character message like "mind if I join?" goes a long way.
- Respect hierarchy in-fiction. If your character is a servant, acting like a lord without earning it is a fast way to get ignored or removed from scenes.
- Find a role by offering labour, not by demanding status. Merchants, farmers, and tradespeople need bodies, and that is how new players typically get drawn into established groups.
- Build a small backstory. A name, a home village, and one goal give other players something to interact with.

None of this is required by the game's code — it is social convention. But in a roleplay-and-progression experience, the social layer is often what determines whether you enjoy it.
